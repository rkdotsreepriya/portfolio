Who I Am &
My Mission

I am an entrepreneur and program manager passionate about designing meaningful educational experiences. I believe education should not merely transfer knowledge—it should transform lives.


Over the years I have built educational institutions, designed curriculum, launched learning products, managed programs, built communities, and led multidisciplinary projects. My strength lies in connecting strategy with execution. I enjoy taking ambitious ideas from whiteboard discussions to real-world implementation.


My Mission: To build education systems that are more accessible, engaging, inclusive, and future-ready. To empower children, educators, and communities through thoughtful design and meaningful programs.
