Hey everyone, 

I recently had the pleasure of attending the CMX Summit 2019 and I wanted to share my experience with you all. For those who aren't familiar, CMX Summit is an annual event for community builders, hosted by CMX - a platform for community professionals. 

The summit took place over two days and was jam-packed with amazing speakers, networking opportunities, and interactive sessions. The theme for this year's summit was "The Rise of Community" and it definitely lived up to its name. 

One of the things I loved about the summit was the diversity of speakers and topics. There were community leaders from a variety of industries and backgrounds who shared their insights and experiences on building and scaling communities. The sessions covered everything from community management and engagement, to community health and sustainability. 

The keynote speakers were fantastic and each brought a unique perspective to the table. Some of my favorites were: 

David Spinks, the CEO of CMX, who spoke about the evolution of community and where it's headed in the future. 

Bailey Richardson, a Partner at People & Company, who talked about the power of belonging and how it can be harnessed to build strong communities. 

Richard Millington, the Founder of FeverBee, who shared his insights on community metrics and how to measure the success of a community. 

In addition to the speakers, there were also interactive sessions and workshops that allowed attendees to dive deeper into specific topics. I attended a workshop on community engagement strategies and learned a ton from the facilitators and other attendees. 

But perhaps the best part of the summit was the networking opportunities. I got to meet and connect with so many amazing people who are doing incredible things in their communities. From founders of startups to community managers at large corporations, everyone was so friendly and eager to share their experiences and learn from others. 

Overall, I had an amazing time at CMX Summit 2019 and I left feeling inspired and motivated to continue building and growing communities. If you're a community builder or interested in community building, I highly recommend attending next year's summit. You won't be disappointed! 

Thanks for reading!
