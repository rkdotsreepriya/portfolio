# Speaking on Parental Wellbeing with ASHA Workers at Fort Kochi

I had the opportunity to conduct a workshop on **Parental Wellbeing** for the dedicated **ASHA Workers of Fort Kochi**. It was an engaging session where we discussed the importance of supporting parents' emotional wellbeing, understanding the challenges families face, and the role of early childhood experiences in building healthy communities.

ASHA workers are often the first point of contact for families, making their role in promoting awareness and positive parenting practices incredibly valuable. It was encouraging to see their enthusiasm, thoughtful participation, and commitment to supporting the families they serve.

I extend my sincere gratitude to **WRI India** for inviting me to be part of this meaningful initiative. My special thanks to **Sufiyan** and ***Amy** for the guidance and coordination throughout the program. I also acknowledge the **Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs** for supporting initiatives that strengthen communities through knowledge sharing and capacity building.

It is always a privilege to contribute to programs that empower frontline community workers. 

**Together, every conversation with a parent has the potential to shape a child's future.**
