Hey there, fellow tech enthusiasts!&#x20;

As the founder of Pehia, a non-profit organization that aims to bridge the gender gap in technology, I'm thrilled to introduce you to our latest initiative - the Change Maker Fellowship!

Our mission at Pehia is to create a more inclusive tech community by empowering women to pursue careers in computer science and programming. We believe that everyone deserves a fair shot at success, and we're committed to providing the resources and support needed to make that happen.

That's where the CM Fellowship comes in! Led by our very own[ Kiran Johns ](https://www.linkedin.com/in/kiranjohns/)(who's like a brother to me), the program is a 12-month journey that blends online training with community engagement. We want to help you build your skills and make a real impact on society through tech!

Now, let's talk eligibility. You need to be a resident of Kerala, India, and over 18 years old. You should be in your first or second year of undergrad or pursuing a graduate degree, and you should have a genuine interest in tech community activities. Plus, you should be willing to commit around 10 hours per week to the fellowship.

During the program, you'll get to work on some exciting projects and learn from industry experts. You'll also have the chance to collaborate with other Fellows who share your passion for tech.

We believe that the CM Fellowship is a great opportunity to make a difference in the world while building your own skills and knowledge. So, what are you waiting for? Fill up the[ interest form](https://bit.ly/CM-fellowship-interestform) today and let's create a more inclusive tech community together!
