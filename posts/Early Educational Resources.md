# Early Educational Resources

Some renowned and reputable early educational websites and organizations globally that offer valuable resources, tools, and programs for young learners.

1. **PBS Kids**:

   * **Website**: [PBS Kids](https://pbskids.org/)

   Known globally for its educational content, PBS Kids provides a range of activities, games, and videos based on its renowned TV shows.
2. **Khan Academy Kids**:

   * **Website**: Khan Academy Kids

   Khan Academy's platform for young learners is widely recognized for its comprehensive approach to foundational skills in reading, math, and more.
3. **Sesame Workshop**:

   * **Website**: [Sesame Workshop](https://www.sesameworkshop.org/)

   Originating from the iconic "Sesame Street," Sesame Workshop offers invaluable resources on literacy, numeracy, social skills, and emotional development.
4. **Starfall**:

   * **Website**: [Starfall Education](https://www.starfall.com/)

   Starfall's phonics-based approach to reading has gained significant recognition, making it a go-to resource for many parents and educators.
5. **National Association for the Education of Young Children (NAEYC)**:

   * **Website**: [NAEYC](https://www.naeyc.org/)

   NAEYC's commitment to promoting high-quality early learning places it among the leading organizations advocating for young children's educational needs.
6. **Zero to Three**:

   * **Website**: [Zero to Three](https://www.zerotothree.org/)

   Focusing on the critical developmental years of infants and toddlers, Zero to Three offers research-backed resources and guidance for parents and caregivers.
7. **International Literacy Association (ILA)**:

   * **Website**: [ILA](https://www.literacyworldwide.org/)

   With a global reach, ILA plays a crucial role in advancing literacy education and offers resources for educators working across various age groups.
8. **Common Sense Media**:

   * **Website**: [Common Sense Media](https://www.commonsensemedia.org/)

   While not exclusively for early education, Common Sense Media's reviews and recommendations have become invaluable for parents and educators seeking age-appropriate media resources.

Take a peek when you get a chance! If you think I've missed any awesome resources out there, please let me know. Always open to adding more gems to the list.
